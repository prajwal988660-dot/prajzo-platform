FAQ

Questions Clients Ask Before Signing

Still unsure about something? Ask us directly and a real person replies within one business day.

Ask your question
How long does a typical project take?

Websites take 2–3 weeks, web apps 6–10 weeks and mobile apps 8–12 weeks. Your proposal includes a week-by-week plan and a fixed launch date.

How much does it cost, and how do payments work?

You get a fixed quote before any work starts, then pay in three milestones: 30% to start, 40% when you approve the designs and 30% at launch. No hourly billing and no surprise invoices.

Who owns the code and designs?

You do, from day one. The code lives in your GitHub or GitLab account, the designs in your Figma and the hosting under your own login.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. Send us your NDA or ask for ours, and we sign it within one business day, before you share any details.

I'm not technical. Can you still help?

Yes. Most founders we work with aren't technical. We explain every decision in plain language, and each Friday you click through the latest build yourself.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes free support for bugs and small fixes: 30 days on Starter, 60 on Growth and 90 on Scale. After that you can join a care plan from ₹3,000 a month or hand everything to your own team.

Can you take over an existing product?

Yes. We start with a one-week audit of the code, design and hosting, then give you a fixed plan to fix, rebuild or extend it.

What technology do you use?

React and Next.js for the web, Flutter for mobile, Node.js and PostgreSQL behind them, Shopify for stores and the Claude API for AI features. We choose what fits your product and your team.
